# Understanding Neural Networks: A Beginner's Guide

## Introduction
Neural networks are at the heart of modern artificial intelligence. They power everything from image recognition systems to language translation services. But what exactly are neural networks, and how do they work?

## What is a Neural Network?
A neural network is a computational model inspired by the human brain. It consists of interconnected nodes (neurons) organized in layers. These networks can learn patterns from data through a process called training.

## Basic Components
- **Neurons**: The basic processing units
- **Weights**: Parameters that determine the strength of connections
- **Activation Functions**: Functions that determine whether a neuron should be activated
- **Layers**: Input, hidden, and output layers

## Applications
Neural networks are used in various domains including:
- Image and speech recognition
- Natural language processing
- Autonomous vehicles
- Medical diagnosis

## Conclusion
Neural networks form the foundation of deep learning. Understanding their basic principles is crucial for anyone interested in AI and machine learning.
